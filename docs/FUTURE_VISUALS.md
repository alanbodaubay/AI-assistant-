# Future Visual & Interaction Concepts

## 1. Visual Persona Exploration: The Little Sun vs. The Pixy
- **Concept A: The Little Sun (Preferred)**:
  - Procedural solar corona rays, gentle pulsing solar flares, warm incandescent hues (golden core blending into deep orange and radiant white).
  - Micro-reactions: flares flare outward when excited or celebrating; dims into an evening twilight ember when user is focused or it's late at night.
- **Concept B: The Pixy**:
  - Ethereal winged spirit / bioluminescent fairy with gentle trailing particle dust.

## 2. Ambient Desktop Autonomy (The Devil in the Details)
- **Idle Roaming**:
  - Instead of remaining static in one corner, the companion can occasionally glide across screen borders or perch on top of active application windows.
  - Sits on the titlebar of the active window when you're working.
- **Contextual Curiosity**:
  - Flies near a new window when opened to glance at context before returning to its perch.
  - Dims its glow and perches on the taskbar when a fullscreen video or game is detected.
